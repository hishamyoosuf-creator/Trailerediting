
# Professional Trailer Editing Workflow

## Phase 1 - Understanding the Film

Watch and analyze the available movie footage.

Identify:
- Main characters
- Story conflict
- Main emotional theme
- Mystery elements
- Action moments
- Suspense sequences

## Phase 2 - Trailer Hook

Create an engaging opening within
the first 5-15 seconds.

Possible hook types:
- Powerful dialogue
- Mystery scene
- Unexpected visual
- Emotional moment
- Suspense moment
- Action sequence

## Phase 3 - Story Structure

Suggested trailer structure:

1. Opening Hook
2. Movie Introduction
3. Character Introduction
4. Story Conflict
5. Suspense Build-up
6. Music Escalation
7. Fast Montage
8. Climax
9. Movie Title
10. Release Information

## Phase 4 - Editing

Use DaVinci Resolve.

Focus on:
- Rhythm
- Shot duration
- Music synchronization
- Dialogue placement
- Sound transitions
- Emotional pacing

## Phase 5 - Final Review

Check:
- Story clarity
- Audio levels
- Music synchronization
- Shot continuity
- Text spelling
- Export quality
